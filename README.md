## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a **complete end-to-end data analytics pipeline** applied to a real-world retail business dataset. The goal was to extract meaningful insights from 3,900 customer records and present them in a way that supports **strategic business decision-making.**

The workflow covers every stage a Data Analyst handles on the job:
- 🧹 Raw data → cleaning & EDA (Python)
- 🔍 Structured querying (PostgreSQL)
- 📊 Interactive dashboard (Power BI)
- 📋 Stakeholder presentation (PowerPoint + PDF)

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Gender-Based Revenue Analysis
| Gender | Revenue | Insight |
|--------|---------|---------|
| 👨 Male | $157K | 2× more revenue than female |
| 👩 Female | $75K | Opportunity for targeted campaigns |

> 💡 **Insight:** Male customers generate **2× more revenue** — marketing strategy should include gender-specific targeting

---

### 🚚 Shipping Preference Analysis
| Shipping Type | Avg Spend | Insight |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| ⚡ Express | $2 More | Higher spenders prefer speed |
| 🐢 Standard | Base | Price-sensitive segment |

> 💡 **Insight:** Express shipping users spend more on average — **premium shipping promotions** can boost revenue

---

### 👥 Customer Segmentation
| Segment | Count | Strategy |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 🌟 Loyal Customers | 3,116 | Retention programs |
| 🔄 Non-Subscribers | 73% | Convert to subscribers |
| 🆕 New Customers | Remaining | Onboarding campaigns |

> 💡 **Insight:** 73% are non-subscribers — huge opportunity to **grow subscription revenue**

---

### 📦 Product & Category Performance
- ✅ Identified **top-performing product category** driving majority revenue
- ✅ Found products **most dependent on discounts** to drive purchases
- ✅ Discovered **subscriber vs non-subscriber** spending is nearly identical
- ✅ Uncovered **age-group based** revenue patterns

## 🔄 Process Steps

```
📂 Step 1: Load & Inspect Data (Python)
   ↓  df.info(), df.describe(), df.shape
   
🧹 Step 2: Data Cleaning & EDA (Python)
   ↓  Missing values, feature engineering, standardization
   
🔍 Step 3: SQL Analysis (PostgreSQL)
   ↓  8+ business queries — revenue, segments, shipping
   
📊 Step 4: Dashboard (Power BI)
   ↓  KPI cards, charts, slicers, dynamic filtering
   
📋 Step 5: Report & Presentation
   ↓  PDF report + PowerPoint for stakeholders
   
💡 Step 6: Recommendations
   ↓  4 data-backed business recommendations
```
